## 1. Commission Structure

GradSnap currently charges **0% commission** on bookings made through the
platform. Whatever you set as your price is what you get paid.

GradSnap may introduce a commission or other fees in the future. If it does,
we will tell you in advance, before any change applies to your bookings, and
you will be free to stop using the platform.

GradSnap is not a replacement for your existing marketing — it's an additional
channel to reach graduating students who are actively looking for a
photographer.

## 2. Payments

GradSnap does not process payments and never holds your money — there are no
payouts for us to send you, because the money never passes through us in the
first place. Clients pay you directly.

You choose how you want to get paid, and that choice is shown on your public
profile so clients know what to expect before booking:

- **Stripe** — through a Stripe Payment Link you set up yourself and add to
  your profile settings.
- **Cash** — paid to you in person.

You also decide whether you ask for a deposit to confirm the booking, and how
much.

Since payment happens outside GradSnap, we have no way to verify it
automatically — you mark a booking as paid yourself from your bookings
dashboard once you've actually received the money.

## 3. Cancellations

Photographers must cancel at least 48 hours before a session to avoid
penalties. Repeated last-minute cancellations may result in account
suspension.

## 4. Content

By listing your work on GradSnap you confirm you own or have the right to use
all portfolio images uploaded. GradSnap may use portfolio thumbnails for
promotional purposes.

## 5. Account Suspension

GradSnap reserves the right to suspend or terminate accounts that violate
these terms, engage in fraudulent activity or receive repeated negative
reviews.
